Typist MCP provides secure, read-only access over Streamable HTTP and browser-based OAuth.

- Search or list completed transcripts with `search_transcripts`
- Read transcript text, segments, or subtitles with `read_transcript`
- Create one-hour TXT, SRT, or VTT export URLs with `download_transcript`

Locked transcripts remain discoverable, but their content is limited to the preview available to your account.
